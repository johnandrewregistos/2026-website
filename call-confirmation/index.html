How to prepare

You don't need a polished presentation. Just come ready to talk openly about your business.

  1. 01
    Know your current priorities

    Think about what you're trying to grow and the role content needs to play in that growth.

  2. 02
    Bring your current content examples

    Have your website and social media profiles ready so we can look at your current online presence.

  3. 03
    Identify your biggest bottleneck

    Consider what's holding you back—strategy, planning, filming, editing, publishing, or consistency.

  4. 04
    Be clear about your goals

    Think about what a successful content partnership would help you achieve over the next 3–6 months.
